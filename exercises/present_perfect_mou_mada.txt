# TOPIC: Present Perfect — もう and まだ (Already / Not Yet)
# LEVEL: beginner

# ─────────────────────────────────────────
# Structures:
#   もう (mou) + past affirmative = already did
#   もう (mou) + past question    = have you … yet?
#   まだ (mada) + te-form + いません = have not … yet
# Note: "not yet" uses the NEGATIVE PROGRESSIVE (〜ていません),
#   not simple past negative (〜ませんでした)
# ─────────────────────────────────────────

---
DIR: EN>JP
Q: Have you eaten lunch yet?
ROM: Mou ohirugohan wo tabemashita ka?
JP: もう おひるごはん を たべました か？
HINT: "have you … yet?" = もう (mou) + past affirmative + か？(ka?)

---
DIR: EN>JP
Q: No, I haven't had lunch yet.
ROM: Iie, mada tabete imasen.
JP: いいえ、まだ たべていません。
HINT: "not yet" = まだ (mada) + te-form + いません (imasen) · NOT tabemasendeshita — that means "I didn't eat" with no "yet" nuance
