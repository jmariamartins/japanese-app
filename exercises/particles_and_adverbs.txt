# TOPIC: Particles and Adverbs
# LEVEL: beginner

# ─────────────────────────────────────────
# Particles covered: は, も, の, に/へ, で, を, と, や
# Key distinctions:
#   に = destination / time reference / location of existence
#   で = location of action / means of transport
#   と = and (exhaustive, people or things) / together with
#   や = and/etc. (non-exhaustive list)
#   も = also / too (replaces は or を, never adds to them)
# ─────────────────────────────────────────

---
DIR: EN>JP
Q: I will go to the cafe at 10AM.
ROM: Watashi wa gozen juu-ji ni kafe ni ikimasu.
JP: わたし は ごぜん１０じ に かふぇ に いきます。
HINT: は (wa) = topic · に (ni) = specific time · に/へ (ni/e) = destination · both に here are different uses!

---
DIR: EN>JP
Q: Mary's younger brother studies at the library.
ROM: Mearii no otouto wa toshokan de benkyou shimasu.
JP: めありー の おとうと は としょかん で べんきょうします。
HINT: の (no) = possession (Mary's brother) · は (wa) = topic · で (de) = location of an action (studying)

Q: What did Masato drink?
ROM: Masato wa nani wo nomimashita ka?
JP: まさと は なに を のみました か？
HINT: は (wa) = topic · を (wo) = direct object marker · なに (nani) = what

JP: あなた は あした も がっこう に いきます か？
HINT: も (mo) = also/too · it replaces は (wa) here — not added to it · あした (ashita) takes no particle (floating time word)

DIR: EN>JP
Q: Will you watch a movie as well?
ROM: Anata mo eiga wo mimasu ka?
JP: あなた も えいが を みます か？
HINT: も (mo) = also · replaces は (wa) entirely · do not write は も (wa mo) together
