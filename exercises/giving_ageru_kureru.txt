# TOPIC: I Give… — あげる / くれる (Giving and Receiving Direction)
# LEVEL: intermediate

# ─────────────────────────────────────────
# Structure:
#   [Giver] は/が [Recipient] に [Object] を あげる / くれる
#   Giver = subject (は or が) · Recipient = に
# あげる = giving moves AWAY from the speaker:  I → you → others
#          (I give to you / to others · You give to others · Others give to others)
# くれる = giving moves TOWARD the speaker:     others → you → I
#          (Others give to me / to you · You give to me)
# くれる is also used when somebody very close to you (family, very good friend)
# receives something, because you feel you benefited too.
# Both are ru-verbs: あげます/あげました/あげません/あげませんでした
#                    くれます/くれました/くれません/くれませんでした
# ─────────────────────────────────────────

---
DIR: EN>JP
Q: I will give Maki flowers.
ROM: Watashi wa Maki ni hana o agemasu.
JP: 私は まきに 花を あげます。
HINT: giver 私 (watashi) は (wa) · recipient まき (Maki) に (ni) · object 花 (hana) を (o) · あげる (ageru) because giving moves away from me

JP: ともは さきに 時計を あげました。
HINT: others → others = あげる (ageru) · past: あげました (agemashita) · とけい (tokei) = watch/clock
