て形 — Te-form
te-kei
Conjugation Rules & Usages
Part I
Conjugation Rules — Verbs, Adjectives & Nouns
Type Rule Example
Ru-verb All ru-verbs
(ichidan · ends in -iru / -eru) 		Remove る (ru) from dictionary form → add て (te) 食（た）べる → 食（た）べて taberu → tabete  ·  "to eat"
U-verb Group 1 · ends in う(u)、 つ(tsu)、 る(ru)* Remove final letter → add って (tte) 取（と）る → 取（と）って toru → totte  ·  "to take"
U-verb Group 2 · ends in む(mu)、 ぶ(bu)、 ぬ(nu) Remove final letter → add んで (nde) 死（し）ぬ → 死（し）んで shinu → shinde  ·  "to die"
U-verb Group 3 · ends in く(ku) Remove final letter → add いて (ite) 書（か）く → 書（か）いて kaku → kaite  ·  "to write"
U-verb Group 4 · ends in ぐ(gu) Remove final letter → add いで (ide) 泳（およ）ぐ → 泳（およ）いで oyogu → oyoide  ·  "to swim"
U-verb Group 5 · ends in す(su) Remove final letter → add して (shite) 話（はな）す → 話（はな）して hanasu → hanashite  ·  "to speak"
Irregular する(suru) & くる(kuru) only No pattern — must be memorised する → して suru → shite  ·  "to do" くる → きて kuru → kite  ·  "to come"
Adjectives & Nouns — Te-form for Joining Sentences
い-Adjective All い-adjectives
(i-adjective) 		Replace final い (i) with くて (kute)
Exception: いい(ii) (good) → よくて(yokute) 		やすい → やすくて yasui → yasukute  ·  "cheap" いい → よくて ii → yokute  ·  "good" (irregular)
な-Adjective All な-adjectives
(na-adjective) 		Replace final な (na) with で (de) げんきな → げんきで genkina → genkide  ·  "healthy/energetic" きれいな → きれいで kireina → kireide  ·  "clean/pretty"
Noun + です Any noun sentence
(noun + desu) 		Replace です (desu) with で (de) にほんじんです → にほんじんで nihonjindesu → nihonjinde  ·  "Japanese person"
Part II
Usages
Usage Structure & Meaning Example
Making Requests "Please do … for me."
te-form + ください(kudasai)
 ここに書（か）いてください。 Koko ni kaite kudasai. "Please write it here."
Permission "You may do …"
te-form + もいいです(mo ii desu)
→ granting permission
te-form + もいいですか？(mo ii desu ka?)
→ asking permission: "May I …?"
te-form + はいけません(wa ikemasen)
→ prohibition: "You must not …" 		ここに座（すわ）ってもいいです。 Koko ni suwatte mo ii desu. "You may sit here."
写真（しゃしん）を撮（と）ってもいいですか？ Shashin o totte mo ii desu ka? "May I take a photo?"
ここで食（た）べてはいけません。 Koko de tabete wa ikemasen. "You must not eat here."
Connecting Verbs "Do A, then do B" / manner of action
[verb 1 te-form] + [verb 2 present aff.]
· Verb 1 (te-form) = first action or the manner
· Verb 2 (present affirmative) = sets the tense
· Can chain more than 2 verbs 		バスに乗（の）って仕事（しごと）に行（い）きます。 Basu ni notte shigoto ni ikimasu. "I go to work by bus." (lit. ride the bus and go to work)
Action in Progress "is doing …" (present continuous)
te-form + いる(iru)
Used with verbs describing activities that last (eating, reading, waiting…). The helping verb いる(iru) is conjugated for tense:
AffirmativeNegative
Present いますimasu いませんimasen
Past いましたimashita いませんでしたimasendeshita
スーは今（いま）勉強（べんきょう）しています。 Su- wa ima benkyou shite imasu. "Sue is studying right now."
cf. 勉強します (benkyou shimasu) = "Sue will study" (future/habitual)
Current State "is [state]…" — result of past change
te-form + いる(iru)
Used with verbs describing an instantaneous change (marry, wear, know, live…). ている(te iru) expresses the resulting state, not the act itself.
Common verbs: 知る(shiru)、 着る(kiru)、 住む(sumu)、 持つ(motsu)、 結婚する(kekkon suru)… 		みのりは結婚（けっこん）しています。 Minori wa kekkon shite imasu. "Minori is married." (current status)
みちこはTシャツを着（き）ています。 Michiko wa T-shatsu o kite imasu. "Michiko is wearing a T-shirt." (current state)
Joining Adj. & Nouns "… and …" — linking descriptions
[adj/noun te-form] + [adj/noun present aff.]
Use the te-form of い/な-adjectives and Noun+です(desu) to chain two or more descriptions. 		綺麗（きれい）で、広（ひろ）いです。 kireide, hiroi desu. "[It is] clean and spacious."
けんは日本人（にほんじん）で、学生（がくせい）です。 Ken wa nihonjinde, gakusei desu. "Ken is Japanese and a student."

★ Key rules to remember: (1) When connecting verbs or adjectives, only the last element carries the tense.   (2) ている(te iru) with activity verbs = action in progress; with change-of-state verbs = current result/status.   (3) You can chain more than 2 elements using te-form throughout.
