助詞
Japanese Particles — Complete Reference

Particles marked TRICKY have multiple meanings depending on context

Particle Use Case Example
wa Topic marker Marks what the sentence is about Watashi wa Maria desu. I am Maria.
Contrast Implies "but" or "as for" — contrasting two things Kare wa Tokyo, watashi wa Osaka desu. He is from Tokyo, but I am from Osaka.
ga Subject marker Tricky Marks who/what does the action (emphasis on the subject itself) Imouto ga imasu. I have a little sister. (lit. A little sister exists.)
Conjunction: "but" Tricky Connects two sentences with a contrast Tokyo shusshin desu ga, ima Osaka ni sunde imasu. I'm from Tokyo, but I live in Osaka now.
Object of feelings/ability Used with verbs of liking, wanting, being able to Nihongo ga suki desu. I like Japanese.
o Direct object marker Marks what the action is done to Gohan o tabemasu. I eat rice.
Movement through a place Marks a place one moves through or along Koen o arukimasu. I walk through the park.
no Possessive (like 's) Shows belonging or relationship Watashi no imouto wa 25-sai desu. My little sister is 25.
Nominalizer Turns a verb/adjective into a noun Nihongo o hanasu no ga suki desu. I like speaking Japanese.
ni Direction / destination Where you are going (with motion verbs) Nihon ni ikimasu. I go to Japan.
Location of existence Where something/someone is (with imasu/arimasu) Tokyo ni sunde imasu. I live in Tokyo.
Time marker Marks a specific point in time Shichi-ji ni okimasu. I wake up at 7 o'clock.
e Direction (toward) Similar to ni but emphasizes movement toward; interchangeable with ni in many cases Nihon e ikimasu. I'm heading toward Japan.
de Location of action Where an action takes place Resutoran de tabemasu. I eat at the restaurant.
Means / method How something is done — tool, transport, language Hikoki de ikimasu. I go by airplane.
kara Origin / starting point From a place or time (used with a verb) Porutogaru kara kimashita. I came from Portugal.
Because / therefore Gives a reason (at end of clause) Ame desu kara, ikimasen. Because it's raining, I won't go.
made Until / up to Endpoint in time or space Tokyo made ikimasu. I'm going as far as Tokyo.
to And (exhaustive list) Connects nouns (lists all items) Gohan to mizu o kudasai. Rice and water, please.
With (together with) Doing something with someone Tomodachi to ikimasu. I go with a friend.
ya And (non-exhaustive list) Lists some examples, implying there are more (like "and so on") Hon ya zasshi ga arimasu. There are books, magazines, and so on.
ka Question marker Turns a statement into a question (replaces "?") Nan-sai desu ka? How old are you?
ne Seeking agreement / "right?" Like "isn't it?" — invites the listener to agree Ii tenki desu ne. Nice weather, isn't it?
yo Emphasis / assertion Adds confidence, like "I'm telling you!" or "you know!" Oishii desu yo! It's delicious, I tell you!
mo Also / too / even Replaces wa or ga to add "too" or "also" Watashi mo ikimasu. I am going too.
Tip: wa vs ga — both mark the subject, but wa sets the topic (what we're talking about) while ga focuses on who/what specifically does the action.
