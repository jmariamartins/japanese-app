basho — location sentences
あります (arimasu) · います (imasu) · Position Words · Classes 68–70
Class 68
〜が あります — Non-living Things & Events
~ ga arimasu
Use case Examples Important notes
(1) Object at a location
あります · arimasu (Location) に (Object) が あります。
あそこに マクドナルドが あります。 Asoko ni Makudonarudo ga arimasu. "There is a McDonald's over there."
あそこに サーフボードが あります。 Asoko ni sa-fubo-do ga arimasu. "There is a surfboard over there."
  • Use に (ni) — not で (de) — for the location.
  • Location comes at the start of the sentence.
  • Object is followed by が (ga) — not は (wa).
(2) Ownership — "I have something"
(Location) に (Object) が あります。location = where you keep it
家に 冷蔵庫が あります。 Ie ni reizouko ga arimasu. "I have a fridge at home."
時間が ありますか？ Jikan ga arimasu ka? "Do you have time?"
  • Same formula as (1); the location can be omitted if obvious.
  • English says "have/own" but Japanese treats it as the object existing at a place.
(3a) Event — no time or location specified
(Event) が あります。
日本語の クラスが あります。 Nihongo no kurasu ga arimasu. "I have a Japanese lesson."
  • Think of it as "There is a [lesson / party / test]."
(3b) Event — with a time reference
(Day/Time) に (Event) が あります。
火曜日に テストが あります。 Kayoubi ni tesuto ga arimasu. "I have a test on Tuesday."
金曜日に パーティーが あります。 Kinyoubi ni pa-ti- ga arimasu. "I have a party on Friday."
  • Use に (ni) after specific days and times — e.g. 月曜日に(getsuyoubi ni), 三時に(sanji ni)…
  • Exception: floating time words do not take に — they stand alone:
    明日(ashita), 今日(kyou), 来週(raishuu) → no particle needed.
(3c) Event — with a location
(Location) で (de) (Event) が あります。
学校で テストが あります。 Gakkou de tesuto ga arimasu. "I have a test at school."
  • ⚠ Switch to で (de) — not に — when describing where an event takes place.
  • で is the normal "action/event location" particle — rules revert to standard here.
★ に (ni) vs で (de) summary for あります (arimasu):
Object/person existing at a place → に (ni)  ·  Event taking place at a location → で (de)  ·  Specific time reference → に (ni)  ·  Floating time words 明日(ashita), 今日(kyou), 来週(raishuu) → no particle
Class 69
〜が います — Living Beings
~ ga imasu
Use case Examples Important notes
People, animals and any sentient/living being
います · imasu (Location) に (Person/Animal) が います。
あそこに 留学生が います。 Asoko ni ryuugakusei ga imasu. "There is an international student over there."
あそこに 猫が います。 Asoko ni neko ga imasu. "There is a cat over there."
あそこに 私のスペイン語の先生が います。 Asoko ni watashi no supeingo no sensei ga imasu. "My Spanish teacher is over there."
  • Use います (imasu) for people, animals, and any living being.
  • Use あります (arimasu) for non-living things, objects, and events.
  • Same structure as あります (1): に (ni) for location, location first, が (ga) before subject.
★ あります (arimasu) vs います (imasu) at a glance:
Non-living objects & events → あります (arimasu)  ·  People, animals, living beings → います (imasu)
Class 70
Describing Where Things Are
Use case Examples Important notes
Asking where something is
どこ · doko (Object) は どこ ですか？wa doko desu ka?
車は どこ ですか？ Kuruma wa doko desu ka? "Where is the car?"
  • Note the switch to は (wa) — the object is the topic being asked about.
  • Short answers:
    ここ(koko) = here  ·  そこ(soko) = right there  ·  あそこ(asoko) = over there
Stating where something is — two valid forms
(Object) は (Landmark) の (position) です (desu)。 (Object) は (Landmark) の (position) に あります (arimasu)。more natural in speech for places & buildings
銀行は 図書館の となり です。 Ginkou wa toshokan no tonari desu. "The bank is next to the library."
傘は 机の下 です。 Kasa wa tsukue no shita desu. "The umbrella is under the table."
病院は パン屋の前に あります。 Byouin wa panya no mae ni arimasu. "The hospital is in front of the bakery."
車は 自転車と家の間 です。 Kuruma wa jitensha to ie no aida desu. "The car is between the bike and the house."
  • Object is the topic → は (wa).
  • です (desu) version: treats the location as a noun predicate. More formal / written.
  • に あります (ni arimasu) version: "the thing exists at that location." More natural in speech.
  • Both forms are correct.
  • For "between A and B": A と (to) B の 間(aida).
Location words with an action verb
(Landmark) の (position) で (de) (verb)。
カフェの前で 待ちます。 Kafe no mae de machimasu. "I will wait in front of the cafe."
駅の中で 待ちます。 Eki no naka de machimasu. "I will wait inside the station."
  • When a location word precedes an action verb, use で (de) as the location particle — not に (ni).
Position vocabulary
〜の前 (まえ) ~ no mae in front of
〜の後ろ (うしろ) ~ no ushiro behind
〜の横 (よこ) ~ no yoko beside / next to
〜の隣 (となり) ~ no tonari next to (adjacent)
〜の左 (ひだり) ~ no hidari to the left of
〜の右 (みぎ) ~ no migi to the right of
〜の中 (なか) ~ no naka inside
〜の外 (そと) ~ no soto outside
〜の上 (うえ) ~ no ue on / above
〜の下 (した) ~ no shita under / below
〜の側 (そば) ~ no soba by / near
A と B の間 (あいだ) A to B no aida between A and B
  • All position words attach with の (no) to the reference landmark.
  • よこ (yoko) vs となり (tonari) — となり implies direct, immediate adjacency.
  • そば (soba) is broader than となり — generally close, not necessarily touching.
★ Particle quick-reference:
に(ni) → thing/person existing at a place (あります/います)  ·  で(de) → action or event happening at a place  ·  は(wa) → topic (what you're describing the location of)  ·  と(to) → linking two landmarks in "between A と B の間(aida)"
