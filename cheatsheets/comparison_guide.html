Comparison

比較 (hikaku) — comparative & superlative patterns
Grammar · Intermediate
Key rule Japanese adjectives do not change form for comparisons — no "greater / greatest" equivalent. Comparison is expressed by adding particles and words around the nouns being compared. The adjective stays the same.
Type 1

A のほうが B より (property) です。 no houga · yori
