つもりです

tsumori desu  ·  "I intend / plan to do…"
Grammar · Intermediate

Concept & Formation

Rules
Meaning Expresses a plan or intention for the future. The verb before つもり stays in the present tense short form; つもりです itself carries the tense of the sentence.
Verb (present short) + つもりです → "I intend to [verb]"

The Negative — Where Does "Not" Go?

Key Rule
Plan NOT to do → negate the verb
Verb (neg. short) + つもりです
"intend not to do"
e.g. こない つもりです
Do NOT intend → negate つもり
Verb (aff. short) + つもりじゃないです
"don't intend to do"
e.g. くる つもりじゃないです
