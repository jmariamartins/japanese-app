Pronoun  の

の — Noun & Adjective Substitute
Grammar · Beginner

What is Pronoun の?

Concept
Key Rule

When a noun follows an adjective (or another noun with の), and it is clear from context what you are referring to, you can replace that noun with の — functioning like the English pronoun "one". This avoids repetition.

Formation Patterns

Pattern

The original noun is dropped and の takes its place, regardless of whether the modifier is an い-adjective, な-adjective, or a noun with の.

い-adjective + noun ⇒ い-adjective + の
な-adjective + noun ⇒ な-adjective + の
Noun1 の Noun2 ⇒ Noun1 の _____   (の alone, Noun2 omitted)
Noun + の

When a noun follows another noun linked by の, the second noun can simply be omitted, leaving Noun1 の standing alone to mean "Noun1's [one]".

Examples

Examples
Example 1 — い-adjective の
"I have a black sweater. I have a red one, too."
黒(くろ)い セーター を 持(も)っています。
kuroi se-ta- o motteimasu.
I have a black sweater.
赤(あか)い の も 持(も)っています。
akai no mo motteimasu.
I have a red one, too.  (の = sweater)
Example 2 — い-adjective の
"I went to buy a cheap dictionary, but there were no good ones."
安(やす)い 辞書(じしょ) を 買(か)いに行(い)きました。
yasui jisho o kai ni ikimashita.
I went to buy a cheap dictionary.
でも いい の が ありませんでした。
demo ii no ga arimasendeshita.
But there were no good ones.  (の = 辞書)
Example 3 — Noun の (possessive context)
(In answer to "Is this Sue's bag?")  "No, that is Mary's."
いいえ、 それ は メアリー の です。
iie, sore wa Mearii no desu.
No, that is Mary's.  (の = bag / バッグ omitted)
Example 4 — Noun の Noun reduced
"American ice cream is more delicious than Japanese one."
アメリカ の アイスクリーム のほうが 日本(にほん) の より おいしいです。
Amerika no aisukuri-mu no houga Nihon no yori oishiidesu.
American ice cream is more delicious than Japanese one.  (日本の = Japanese ice cream)
Note

アメリカ の アイスクリーム = "Noun1 の Noun2" (full form)  |  日本 の = "Noun1 の ___" (Noun2 / アイスクリーム dropped)

Remember

の as a pronoun is only natural when the noun being replaced is clear from context. When in doubt, keep the full noun to avoid ambiguity.
