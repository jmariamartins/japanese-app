〜でしょう

deshou  ·  "probably" — guess or prediction
Grammar · Intermediate

Concept

Rules
Meaning でしょう is a sentence-final expression meaning "probably". It is used when making a guess, prediction, or cautious assertion. It attaches directly to short forms — no な, の, or だ before it.

Variants

Usage
Formal · Statement 〜でしょう ~ deshou
"probably…" — prediction or guess
Formal · Question 〜でしょうか ~ deshou ka
Invites the listener's guess or opinion
Casual · Confirmation 〜でしょ? ~ desho?
"right?" — seeks agreement on what was just said
Short form The short / casual form of でしょう is だろう (darou). Use it when cautiously phrasing a prediction or analysis in casual speech or inside a larger sentence (e.g. with と思います).
