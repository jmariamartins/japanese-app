〜てみる

verb て-form + みる  ·  "to try doing…"
Grammar · Intermediate

Formation

Rules
Key Rule Use the て-form of a verb + the helping verb みる to express doing something tentatively: "trying something". You are not sure what the outcome will be, but you do it and see what effect it has.
Verb て-form+みる →"try doing [verb]"
About みる みる comes from the verb 見(み)る (to see / look / watch) and conjugates as a regular ru-verb. Here it is written in hiragana, and it means "try", not "see".

Useful pattern When someone says something was good, reply じゃあ、〜てみます ("then I'll try it"), or 〜てみたいです ("I'd like to try it").
