なら

Noun + なら  ·  limitation & contrast: "X, at least / only if…"
Grammar · Intermediate

Core idea

Rules
Key Rule A statement of the form "Noun A なら Predicate X" says that predicate X applies only to A and is not more generally valid. The main ideas of a なら sentence are "limitation" and "contrast".
Noun A+なら positive predicate→X is true of A (only)
Positive statement なら introduces a sentence that says something "positive" about the item being contrasted. The other item usually follows with は and a negative statement.
Aなら〜が、 Bは〜ません。 →A yes, but B no

Examples

Examples
Example 1 · contrast
Q: ブラジルに行(い)ったことがありますか？ (Have you ever been to Brazil?)
"I've been to Mexico, but I've never been to Brazil."
メキシコMekishiko
ならnara
行(い)ったことがありますが、itta koto ga arimasu ga,
Positive item · なら
ブラジルBurajiru
はwa
行(い)ったことがありません。itta koto ga arimasen.
Contrasted item · は + negative
Example 2 · limitation
Q: 日本語(にほんご)がわかりますか？ (Do you understand Japanese?)
"If it is in hiragana, yes."
ひらがなhiragana
ならnara
わかります。wakarimasu.
Only hiragana: understanding is limited to it

How to answer with なら

Pattern
Method Look at the question and the cue. Put the item you can say yes to before なら, then give the item you cannot say yes to with は and a negative predicate.
Practice example · cue: Saturday: No, Sunday: Yes
Q: 土曜日(どようび)はひまですか？ (Are you free on Saturday?)
"I will be free on Sunday, but I am not free on Saturday."
日曜日(にちようび)nichiyoubi
ならnara
ひまですが、hima desu ga,
Yes item
土曜日(どようび)doyoubi
はwa
ひまじゃありません。hima ja arimasen.
No item
