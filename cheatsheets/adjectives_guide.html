I-ADJECTIVES — rule: keep stem, swap ending  |  present aff: -i desu  |  present neg: -ku nai desu  |  past aff: -katta desu  |  past neg: -ku nakatta desu
atsui hot stem: atsu- atsui desu あついです Kono heya wa atsui desu.
This room is hot. 		atsuku nai desu あつくないです Kono heya wa atsuku nai desu.
This room is not hot. 		atsukatta desu あつかったです Kono heya wa atsukatta desu.
This room was hot. 		atsuku nakatta desu あつくなかったです Kono heya wa atsuku nakatta desu.
This room was not hot.
yasashii kind / gentle stem: yasashi- yasashii desu やさしいです Kare wa yasashii desu.
He is kind. 		yasashiku nai desu やさしくないです Kare wa yasashiku nai desu.
He is not kind. 		yasashikatta desu やさしかったです Kare wa yasashikatta desu.
He was kind. 		yasashiku nakatta desu やさしくなかったです Kare wa yasashiku nakatta desu.
He was not kind.
NA-ADJECTIVES — rule: adjective unchanged, desu endings handle tense  |  present neg: ja nai desu  |  past aff: deshita  |  past neg: ja nakatta desu
shinsetsu kind / friendly na-adj (noun origin) shinsetsu desu しんせつです Kanojo wa shinsetsu desu.
She is kind. 		shinsetsu ja nai desu しんせつじゃないです Kanojo wa shinsetsu ja nai desu.
She is not kind. 		shinsetsu deshita しんせつでした Kanojo wa shinsetsu deshita.
She was kind. 		shinsetsu ja nakatta desu しんせつじゃなかったです Kanojo wa shinsetsu ja nakatta desu.
She was not kind.
suki (na) liked / favourite X wa Y ga suki desu Y ga suki desu Yがすきです Watashi wa koohii ga suki desu.
I like coffee. 		Y ga suki ja nai desu Yがすきじゃないです Watashi wa koohii ga suki ja nai desu.
I don't like coffee. 		Y ga suki deshita Yがすきでした Watashi wa koohii ga suki deshita.
I liked coffee. 		Y ga suki ja nakatta desu Yがすきじゃなかったです Watashi wa koohii ga suki ja nakatta desu.
I didn't like coffee.
kirai (na) disliked X wa Y ga kirai desu Y ga kirai desu Yがきらいです Watashi wa natto ga kirai desu.
I dislike natto. 		Y ga kirai ja nai desu Yがきらいじゃないです Watashi wa natto ga kirai ja nai desu.
I don't dislike natto. 		Y ga kirai deshita Yがきらいでした Watashi wa natto ga kirai deshita.
I disliked natto. 		Y ga kirai ja nakatta desu Yがきらいじゃなかったです Watashi wa natto ga kirai ja nakatta desu.
I didn't dislike natto.
IRREGULAR ADJECTIVE — ii (good): present aff uses "ii" but ALL other forms switch to stem "yo-"  |  suki/kirai have special intensified forms: daisuki / daikirai
ii / yoi good ⚠ stem switches to yo- ii desu いいです Tenki wa ii desu.
The weather is good. 		yoku nai desu よくないです Tenki wa yoku nai desu.
The weather is not good. 		yokatta desu よかったです Tenki wa yokatta desu.
The weather was good. 		yoku nakatta desu よくなかったです Tenki wa yoku nakatta desu.
The weather was not good.
Neither like nor dislike: When you neither like nor dislike something, say suki demo kirai demo arimasen (すきでもきらいでもありません) — literally "it is neither liked nor disliked."

suki / kirai as noun modifiers: Add な (na) before a noun — watashi no sukina terebi (わたしのすきなテレビ) = my favourite TV programme.

suki / kirai Special intensified forms totemo suki / totemo kirai とてもすき / とてもきらい Takeshi-san wa koohii ga totemo suki desu.
Takeshi likes coffee very much. 		chotto suki / chotto kirai ちょっとすき / ちょっときらい Watashi wa natto ga chotto kirai desu.
I slightly dislike natto. 		daisuki / daikirai だいすき / だいきらい Takeshi-san wa koohii ga daisuki desu.
Takeshi loves coffee.

Watashi wa natto ga daikirai desu.
I hate natto.
Degree expression placement: totemo and chotto always go directly before the adjective — totemo atsui desu (very hot), never after. The dai- prefix is special to suki/kirai only — it fuses directly with the word: dai + suki = daisuki (love), dai + kirai = daikirai (hate). Both daisuki desu and totemo suki desu are correct and interchangeable for "like very much."
Tip: For i-adjectives all tense changes happen in the ending. For na-adjectives the adjective itself never changes — only desu does the work!
