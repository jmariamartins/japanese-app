Action Verbs — endings are identical across all types once in masu-stem
RU-verb Group 2
taberu (たべる) to eat — drop -ru + masu 		tabemasu たべます I eat / will eat. tabemasen たべません I don't eat. tabemashita たべました I ate. tabemasen deshita たべませんでした I didn't eat.
U-verb Group 1
iku (いく) to go — -ku → -ki + masu 		ikimasu いきます I go / will go. ikimasen いきません I don't go. ikimashita いきました I went. ikimasen deshita いきませんでした I didn't go.
Irregular suru
(する) to do — suru → shi 		shimasu します I do / will do. shimasen しません I don't do. shimashita しました I did. shimasen deshita しませんでした I didn't do.
Irregular kuru
(くる) to come — kuru → ki 		kimasu きます I come / will come. kimasen きません I don't come. kimashita きました I came. kimasen deshita きませんでした I didn't come.
Key pattern to remember: The present tense in Japanese also covers the future — tabemasu means both "I eat" (habit) and "I will eat" (future intention). Context makes it clear which is meant! Also note that ja nai desu (casual) and ja arimasen (formal) are interchangeable — same meaning, different register.
Tip: the endings do all the work — the noun or verb stem never changes across tense or polarity!
